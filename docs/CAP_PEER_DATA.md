# Capgemini peer-data review

Capgemini's first strict H1 2026 peer pass uses Cognizant, Sopra Steria and EPAM as period-aligned peers.

Accenture, CGI and Infosys remain useful business-model comparators, but their latest releases cover different fiscal-quarter periods and are therefore context-only for a strict H1 score.

## Strictly comparable H1 classes with at least three peers

- reported revenue growth
- company-adjusted operating margin
- company-adjusted EPS growth

Free-cash-flow margin currently has only two aligned peer observations (Cognizant and Sopra Steria) in addition to Capgemini, because EPAM does not publish a directly comparable H1 company-defined free-cash-flow metric in this pass.

## Important caveat

Capgemini's 11.3% constant-currency H1 growth includes a material acquisition contribution. It is therefore stored but not treated as equivalent to organic growth.

The absolute Fundamental Quality score remains blocked until weighted comparable-metric coverage reaches the configured 70% threshold.
