# Palomar specialty P&C peer data review

Palomar has a materially stronger peer universe than Admiral.

The H1 2026 dataset contains:
- Kinsale Capital
- Skyward Specialty
- Heritage Insurance
- RLI
- HCI as context where metric definitions do not align

Strictly comparable H1 classes now have at least three peers for:
- combined ratio
- loss ratio
- operating/adjusted ROE
- gross written premium growth
- operating/adjusted EPS growth

The dataset preserves GAAP vs adjusted definitions and blocks HCI metrics where the basis is not directly comparable.

A final Fundamental Quality score is still deferred until component weights for specialty P&C insurers are formally approved.
