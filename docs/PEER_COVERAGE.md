## Meaning of "complete"

Complete means:
- target fundamentals are linked to a primary source;
- the model-curated peer universe has been examined;
- available comparable peer facts are stored with period/definition provenance;
- mismatches are explicitly blocked rather than filled or converted to zero;
- a peer-data status exists.

It does **not** mean all 23 companies are entitled to a Fundamental Quality score. The 70% weighted metric-coverage gate and peer-comparability rules still apply.
